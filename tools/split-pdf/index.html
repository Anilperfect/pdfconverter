How Split PDF works

  • Accepted files: application/pdf,.pdf
  • Output: PDF or ZIP
  • Where it runs: entirely inside your browser - your files are never uploaded
  • Maximum file size: 25 MB

Everything happens locally, so conversion speed depends on your device and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
